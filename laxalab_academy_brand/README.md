# LaxaLab Academy Visual Identity Package

## Adopted identity architecture
- Official institutional name: LaxaLab Academy.
- Primary name signature: the approved laxalab wordmark plus the separate ACADEMY descriptor.
- Horizontal and stacked Academy lockups are supplied in primary, reverse, and monochrome colourways.
- Compact applications and avatars use the independent LAXA mark alone.
- Educational feature imagery may use the approved LAXA + LAB / ACADEMY endorsement lockup.
- Do not add LAB or ACADEMY to 32–48 px icons; use LAXA alone for legibility.
- The custom laxalab wordmark and both graduation caps remain unchanged.
- LAXA assets are transparent; social avatars use a controlled warm-white field rather than the former navy square.
- Primary Navy: #12305A
- Accent Gold: #C6A15B
- Warm White: #FAFAF7
- Mist: #E9EEF4
- Charcoal: #202936
